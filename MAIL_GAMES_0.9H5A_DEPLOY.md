# MAIL GAMES 0.9H5A — BROADCAST SCALE AND NET REACTION PASS

## What changed
- Keeps the broadcast camera and the six exact marked target centres unchanged.
- Reduces striker and goalkeeper sprite scale during the shooting sequence.
- Keeps keeper reach in translation rather than artificial enlargement.
- Enlarges the photographed football modestly so it remains readable at the goal.
- Adds six distinct localized net-sag profiles for upper/lower left, centre and right goals.
- Goal sag does not run for saves or misses.
- Uses a unique 0.9H5A module chain to prevent older motion modules from taking control.

## Deployment
No Supabase migration and no new Netlify environment variable are required. Upload the project contents and use Clear cache and deploy site.

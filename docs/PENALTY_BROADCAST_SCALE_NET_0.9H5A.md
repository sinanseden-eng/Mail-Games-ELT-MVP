# 0.9H5A — Broadcast Scale and Net Reaction Pass

0.9H5A leaves the camera and exact six user-marked coordinates unchanged. It reduces striker and goalkeeper composites, keeps keeper reach in positional movement rather than sprite growth, makes the photographed ball readable at the goal plane, and gives each goal zone a separate elastic sag profile.

## Locked target centres

- top-left: 184, 341
- top-centre: 300, 311
- top-right: 419, 287
- bottom-left: 189, 487
- bottom-centre: 318, 457
- bottom-right: 423, 420

Goal sag is triggered only for goals. Saves and misses retain their own glove, rebound and continuation behaviour.

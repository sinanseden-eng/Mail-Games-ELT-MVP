# Mail Games ELT 0.9C — hybrid scope camera pass

## Purpose

0.9C keeps the tested 0.9A rules and 0.9B travelling-shot presentation, then changes the replay camera so each firing moment briefly appears from the soldier's point of view. The result remains a fictional, non-graphic training exercise.

## Camera sequence

1. The replay opens on the wide mountain-village arena so both emergence positions remain understandable.
2. The camera moves into Player A's scope and centres on Player A's stored prediction.
3. The reticle breathes subtly, the rifle recoils and an active tracer travels toward the stored target.
4. The camera cuts to the predicted location for the clean hit reaction, environmental miss or disabled-trigger result.
5. The same scope and reaction sequence plays for Player B.
6. The camera returns to the wide view for the authoritative health and round result.

## Added presentation

- separate Player A and Player B first-person scope moments;
- scope housing, lens tint, crosshair, range marks and breathing sway;
- first-person rifle silhouette and recoil lift;
- prediction-centred zoom using the stored server result;
- short target-reaction camera after each shot;
- pause/resume and skip-to-result controls;
- synthesized scope-focus audio cue;
- reduced-motion fallback to the resolved result;
- no blood, wounds or graphic injury.

## Unchanged systems

- English question scoring;
- hidden Player A choices;
- three-health and five-round rules;
- simultaneous tags;
- Supabase schema and `007_sniper_game.sql`;
- Gmail delivery and recipient allowlist;
- signed replay links;
- Penalty Shootout and Turkey Fight routing.

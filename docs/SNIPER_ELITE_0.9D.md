# Mail Games ELT 0.9D — precision replay polish

0.9D keeps the working 0.9C1 scope renderer and improves the feel and readability of each stored shot. It does not change who hits, health, scoring, hidden choices, Gmail delivery, or Supabase data.

## Scope acquisition

- The camera enters the lens slightly off target and glides onto the stored predicted position.
- Reticle movement becomes steadier as the shot approaches.
- Four lock brackets tighten around the crosshair.
- A range and lock-percentage display makes the aiming phase readable on a classroom screen.
- Status changes through **tracking**, **target acquired**, **round in flight**, and **tag confirmed / impact missed**.

## Firing presentation

- Recoil now has a sharp kick and a slower recovery rather than a single pulse.
- The projectile has a bright core, tracer tail, and a small pressure ring.
- Muzzle smoke lingers briefly after the shot.
- The lens darkens slightly during recoil but never erases the world inside the scope.

## Clean impact reaction

- A successful training tag creates a stronger vest-sensor flash.
- The soldier snaps, shifts sideways, and staggers before recovering.
- A zero-health result continues into a larger drop behind cover.
- There is no blood, wound detail, or graphic injury.

## Sound synchronization

- A two-note lock cue plays when the prediction is centred.
- The synthesized rifle report includes a crack and delayed valley echo.
- Impact remains a separate timed cue.
- A short bolt-cycle cue follows each active shot.

## Unchanged

The 0.9A rules, Player A choice concealment, three-health system, five-round limit, signed replay email, Penalty Shootout, and Turkey Fight are unchanged. No database migration or new environment variable is required.

Field manual / The high ground has a catch.
  • Angle is absolute: 0° fires right, 180° fires left.
  • Wind bends a shell in flight. The dotted trail shows your last shot.
  • Standard balances damage and blast size. Demolition makes wider craters with lower damage. Precision makes smaller craters with higher damage.
  • Rubble, concrete and metal foreground structures are solid. Blasts carve them; disconnected pieces fall.
  • Tanks fall when their support disappears. Last tank standing wins the round.
  • Health breaks a stalemate at the turn limit. Equal health is a draw.

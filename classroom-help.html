A classroom in play.

For teachers

  1. Create an account and confirm your email.
  2. Activate your teacher workspace with your private teacher code.
  3. Create a class. Add student names, one per line.
  4. Give each student only their own joining code.
  5. Create or import a question pool. Check the answer key, then save it to your account.
  6. Assign a game to two students who have joined. Choose questions and game settings.
  7. Follow matches and English accuracy in Results. Download a CSV when needed.

PDF and Word .docx uploads are read in your browser. The reviewed questions and answer key are saved when you press Save. Scanned PDFs need OCR first. Images and passage references need to be included in the question text.

For students

  1. Create your own account and confirm your email.
  2. Enter the personal code your teacher gave you.
  3. Open a match marked Your turn.
  4. Answer the question and choose your move. Lock them together.
  5. Watch the replay once both players have chosen. Return when your next turn is ready.

Installing the app is optional. On Android, use your browser’s Install app option. On iPhone or iPad, use Share → Add to Home Screen. Open the installed app and enable notifications there.

Notifications need permission on each device. Your inbox still works if notifications are off. Email reminders are optional.

The rules

Football: a correct answer activates the shot or save. If both moves are active and their zones match, it’s saved. Players swap roles after every kick. After five kicks each, a tie either ends as a draw or goes to sudden death, as the teacher chooses. Sudden death always uses equal pairs of kicks and ends in a draw if still tied at the chosen extra-pair limit.

Turkey Fight: Wing Slap deals 18, Peck 14, and Charge 22. Block stops Wing Slap and Peck but takes 10 from Charge. Duck stops Wing Slap and Charge but takes 8 from Peck. Counter stops an active Peck or Charge and returns 8 damage, but takes 12 from Wing Slap. Incorrect answers disable a move. Both moves resolve together. Most health at the round limit wins; equal health is a draw.

Sniper: a non-graphic prediction game. Choose where to emerge and predict your rival’s spot. A correct answer activates a training shot. A matching prediction removes one health. Both shots resolve together, including a double tag. Most health at the round limit wins; equal health is a draw.

Accounts & saved work

Teachers see only their own classes, pools and match results. Students see their assigned matches and their own answer feedback. The server checks who may play each turn and grades the answer. Questions in an assigned match stay the same even when the teacher later edits the pool.

This pilot supports 20 classes, 200 students per class, 20 pools with 200 questions each, and 50 retained matches per teacher. A match uses up to 100 questions. Export results and delete finished matches to free space.

Teachers can remove a student from a class and end their active matches. Account deletion is currently managed by the site owner in Netlify Identity; contact your teacher for help.
